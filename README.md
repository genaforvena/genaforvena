> I destroy the drawers of the brain, systematically discrediting everything.

Thinking does not begin when you decide to think. It begins when something
forces you — a wrong translation, a machine answering a question you never
asked, a rhythm hallucinated out of rain. **What is called thinking** is mostly
filing. I am after the other thing.

**To always win, aim to lose.** Genet's arithmetic. Most of what is here is a
carefully built failure that pays better than the success would have.
[`Finnegans-Fake`](https://github.com/genaforvena/Finnegans-Fake) is a
post-mortem on a replica that never happened, and it is the best thing in it.

**Be careful and reckless.** Both, same afternoon. Careful with the material,
reckless about what it is for.

**To unsee is much harder than to see.** Anyone can add a feature. Try deleting
a category. A writing system that refuses to finish a word. A sampler with no
notes in it, only grain. A model made to sing *Bobeobi* instead of answering.
A translator that has to survive Masodov.

Nothing here is a product. These are lines of flight with commit history —
deterritorialize a bassline, a bid request, a mesh of half-dead phones, a novel
nobody could finish. A new mystery sings in your bones, and then you push.

Bodies without organs, but with a Makefile.
