### Capture a flow, cut it, make it flee

- [**grainneukeln**](https://github.com/genaforvena/grainneukeln) — granular sampler. Fits a beat to any input, including inputs that have none — rain, a voice memo, white noise — then rebuilds the material grain by grain against that imagined grid.
- [**screwdriver**](https://github.com/genaforvena/screwdriver) — real-time terminal workbench for chopping and screwing audio.
- [**funky_to_ashes_voice**](https://github.com/genaforvena/funky_to_ashes_voice) — separates the instrumental bleeding through a voice and keeps the bleed.
- [**bass_taber**](https://github.com/genaforvena/bass_taber) — listens to a track and writes down the bassline as tab.
- [**money_drums**](https://github.com/genaforvena/money_drums) — electronic-acoustic percussion.

### Make language stutter

- [**skiffs**](https://github.com/genaforvena/skiffs) — modular language-model architecture built to make Khlebnikov's *Bobeobi* sing.
- [**the_soft_delerizome_machine**](https://github.com/genaforvena/the_soft_delerizome_machine_a_thousand_guattaris) — GPT-2 fine-tuned into a thousand Guattaris.
- [**translator**](https://github.com/genaforvena/translator) — LLM pipeline translating Russian novels. The target is Masodov.
- [**hidden_language_of_silence**](https://github.com/genaforvena/hidden_language_of_silence) — a writing system that is incomplete on purpose.
- [**Finnegans-Fake**](https://github.com/genaforvena/Finnegans-Fake) — post-mortem on a failed LLM replica of *Finnegans Wake*.
- [**my_favourite_voice_typing**](https://github.com/genaforvena/my_favourite_voice_typing) — voice typing that preserves raw speech instead of tidying it.

### Turn the instruments around on the control society

- [**self-adint**](https://github.com/genaforvena/self-adint) — reads your own advertising profile from the demand side: what a real-time bid request hands a buyer about your phone, for free.
- [**watching_u_watching**](https://github.com/genaforvena/watching_u_watching) — correspondence-study framework for auditing bias in the systems that sort people.
- [**ghost_hosts**](https://github.com/genaforvena/ghost_hosts) — tracks employers advertising jobs that do not exist.
- [**hh-auto-responder**](https://github.com/genaforvena/hh-auto-responder) — answers the hiring funnel at its own speed.

### Assemble a body from junk

- [**lte-workstation**](https://github.com/genaforvena/lte-workstation) — an autopoietic kefir for computing machines: dead phones and one desktop wired into a single organism that senses, coordinates and repairs itself.
- [**mesh-uxn-core**](https://github.com/genaforvena/mesh-uxn-core) — that organism's reflexes as tiny Uxn ROMs, byte-identical on x86 and a 32-bit phone.

### Lines of flight

- [**drunk-walker**](https://github.com/genaforvena/drunk-walker) — blind graph traversal of Google Street View.
- [**coltranize**](https://github.com/genaforvena/coltranize) — browser extension that Coltranizes the chords on any page.
- [**tiny-deep-diver**](https://github.com/genaforvena/tiny-deep-diver) — cuts a long video down to the segments that carry it.
- [**natal_nataly**](https://github.com/genaforvena/natal_nataly) — an astrology companion with pripizdenka.
- [**couch-guardian**](https://github.com/genaforvena/couch-guardian) — a game where you keep a man from getting off the sofa.
