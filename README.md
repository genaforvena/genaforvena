> I destroy the drawers of the brain, systematically discrediting everything.

To always win, aim to lose. Be careful and reckless. To unsee is much harder
than to see.

Nothing here is a product. Read down and it tells a story.

---

### 1. It starts in the ear

Thinking does not begin when you decide to think — it begins when something
forces you. Usually a sound.

- [**grainneukeln**](https://github.com/genaforvena/grainneukeln) — a granular sampler that hallucinates a beat in *anything* — rain, a voice memo, white noise — and grinds it into a new track.
- [**screwdriver**](https://github.com/genaforvena/screwdriver) — real-time terminal workbench for chopping and screwing audio.
- [**funky_to_ashes_voice**](https://github.com/genaforvena/funky_to_ashes_voice) — drags the instrumental bleeding through every word out into the open.
- [**bass_taber**](https://github.com/genaforvena/bass_taber) — listens to a track and writes down the bassline.

### 2. Then language stops behaving

Same operation, other material. Feed a machine a book and it starts filing.
Feed it a book that refuses to be filed and you find out what it actually does.

- [**skiffs**](https://github.com/genaforvena/skiffs) — a modular language-model architecture built to make Khlebnikov's *Bobeobi* sing.
- [**the_soft_delerizome_machine**](https://github.com/genaforvena/the_soft_delerizome_machine_a_thousand_guattaris) — GPT-2 fine-tuned into a thousand Guattaris.
- [**translator**](https://github.com/genaforvena/translator) — LLMs put to translating Russian novels. The target is Masodov, which is the point.
- [**hidden_language_of_silence**](https://github.com/genaforvena/hidden_language_of_silence) — a writing system that is incomplete on purpose.
- [**Finnegans-Fake**](https://github.com/genaforvena/Finnegans-Fake) — a post-mortem on a failed replica of *Finnegans Wake*. The failure is the deliverable.

### 3. Something is reading you back

Turn the same instruments around. The systems that sort people are loud about
what they sell you and silent about what they say about you.

- [**self-adint**](https://github.com/genaforvena/self-adint) — read your own advertising profile from the demand side: what an ad-auction bid request hands a buyer about your phone, for free.
- [**watching_u_watching**](https://github.com/genaforvena/watching_u_watching) — a correspondence-study framework for auditing bias in the machines that sort you.
- [**ghost_hosts**](https://github.com/genaforvena/ghost_hosts) — tracking employers advertising jobs that do not exist.
- [**hh-auto-responder**](https://github.com/genaforvena/hh-auto-responder) — answering the hiring funnel at its own speed.

### 4. So build a body out of the junk

If the machines are going to be an environment, own one.

- [**lte-workstation**](https://github.com/genaforvena/lte-workstation) — an autopoietic kefir for computing machines: dead phones and one desktop kept alive as a single organism that senses, coordinates and heals itself.
- [**mesh-uxn-core**](https://github.com/genaforvena/mesh-uxn-core) — its reflexes as tiny Uxn ROMs, byte-identical on x86 and a 32-bit phone.

### 5. And some things are just experiments

- [**drunk-walker**](https://github.com/genaforvena/drunk-walker) — blind graph traversal of Google Street View.
- [**coltranize**](https://github.com/genaforvena/coltranize) — a browser extension that Coltranizes the chords on any page.
- [**natal_nataly**](https://github.com/genaforvena/natal_nataly) — an astrology companion with pripizdenka.
- [**couch-guardian**](https://github.com/genaforvena/couch-guardian) — a game where you stop a man getting off the sofa.
